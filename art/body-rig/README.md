# Body and rig repair — Blender 4.5

The human shoulder cap was overbuilt and upper-arm vertices retained neck influence. The shared correction rounds the cap around the existing articulation, redistributes shoulder/arm weights, eases elbow/knee blends, and makes coincident skin-mask borders share coordinates, weights and normals. Taubin smoothing softens coarse joint contours. Body-surface corrections transfer to the separate fitted garments. A small neck-base overlap reduces the collar seam; shirt collars retain chest skin behind their openings. Equipped waistbands gain 12 mm of radial clearance. Human arms use a rounder biceps profile, a slimmer tapered forearm, and an elbow pivot moved 20 mm outward in the T-pose to balance upper-arm/forearm lengths. The same field and bind correction is applied to the garments. Human and furry bodies use their own left/right bone orientation; bone names and animation axes are retained; only the two human forearm pivots move, by 20 mm along the existing arm axis.

Run outside the world tree. Inputs and outputs are pinned in inputs.json and assets.json. Download input GLBs from the Spawn asset CDN, decode meshopt with glTF Transform, and name them <input-name>-plain.glb in the cache. Blender: `blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python build.py -- <cache>`. The build imports repair.py beside it. Pack/verify with pack.mjs.txt copied as pack.mjs into a folder with @gltf-transform/core, extensions, functions and meshoptimizer. It verifies all source mesh/morph names, expected bind transforms (only the two human elbow pivots move), and packed weight normalization. Upload out/packed binary art using the existing asset API; never put bytes in this repo.

The creator's face, body sliders, facial hair, species and outfits remain. Runtime keeps pelvis skin behind equipped waistbands while hiding underwear. The new STYLE_V refreshes standing players and NPCs. Previous body IDs are retained for saved-look migration.

Validation: matched Blender standing and bent-joint poses on both human bodies, plus the real game's animation clips. Bone/morph/weight checks cover all exported assets. Not every garment, slider extreme or animation combination has a visual review.
