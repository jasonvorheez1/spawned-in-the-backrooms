# VSCO 2 Community Edition strings

Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler.
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE

Violin and cello sustained and spiccato recordings were trimmed, level-matched and encoded for Worldwright Studio. Natural mode selects soft/strong takes by velocity and alternates short-bow takes deterministically. The compact set does not reproduce every live articulation. See strings-provenance.json for source hashes and served references.
