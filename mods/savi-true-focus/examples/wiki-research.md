# Wiki research receipt: reproducible citations, not proof of truth

**Verification date:** 2026-09-13 UTC  
**Outcome:** Research leg verified within the limits below. Three source URLs opened, representing two official wiki pages and one revision-specific view—not three independent authorities.

## Task

Demonstrate how to cite a wiki revision reproducibly while keeping the revision record separate from evidence that its claims are true. This is a worked research sample for Savi True Focus, not an automatic knowledge store or a new global instruction.

## Plan

- Search for official guidance, then open the relevant documentation and policy; use search results only to locate sources.
- Obtain and open a real revision URL, record its identifying details, and separate verified observations from untested assumptions.

**Progress:** The initial narrow policy search returned no results. A broader search located the official policy, which opened successfully. The guide and its revision-specific view also opened; no page-fetch failure was observed.

## Verified findings

1. **Target the revision, not just the page name.** MediaWiki documents links to specific revisions and says revision numbers can be found in a page's edit history. A reproducible citation should identify the selected revision rather than rely only on the current-page URL. [MediaWiki documentation](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Help:PermanentLink)

2. **A real revision citation was checked.** The guide's retrieved footer supplied the URL below; it was copied and opened, not invented. The URL selects revision **8183006**; the returned page was titled *Help:PermanentLink - MediaWiki* and its banner displayed **22:54, 31 January 2026**. A usable citation is: **MediaWiki, “Help:PermanentLink,” revision 8183006, revision time as displayed: 22:54, 31 January 2026; accessed 2026-09-13 UTC.** [Opened revision](https://www.mediawiki.org/w/index.php?title=Help:PermanentLink&oldid=8183006)

3. **Version identification is not factual validation.** The revision view identifies the version returned. English Wikipedia's policy defines verifiability in terms of checking claims against reliable sources, rather than editors' beliefs. These are different checks: a revision link establishes which text was consulted, not whether every claim in it is true. [Revision view](https://www.mediawiki.org/w/index.php?title=Help:PermanentLink&oldid=8183006) · [Wikipedia policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Verifiability)

4. **Check support at the claim level.** The policy's “Responsibility for providing citations” section requires a reliable source that directly supports the contribution and recommends locating support by page number or an appropriate section. For factual research, follow and assess the supporting sources; do not treat the wiki's revision record as a substitute. [Wikipedia policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Verifiability)

## What was not proved

- No unrelated article's factual claims were independently corroborated. The policy documents English Wikipedia's stated standard, not universal compliance or a rule followed by every wiki.
- Future accessibility, perpetual immutability, identical rendering, and deletion or restriction behavior were not tested or guaranteed. “Permanent” does not make a link proof of truth. The revision time above is reproduced as displayed; its timezone was not established by the retrieved banner.
- Only the research leg was demonstrated. No end-to-end workflow execution or additional independent reviewer check is claimed.

## Reusable takeaway

Keep the research question small: search, open a few authoritative sources, copy and reopen an authentic revision URL, and record the title, revision, access date, and claim-to-source mapping. Separate **what the wiki recorded** from **what supporting evidence establishes**. If a check fails, report it, change the approach, and retain explicit uncertainty. An independent reviewer can help check a separable claim-to-source match; count that review only if it actually happens.
