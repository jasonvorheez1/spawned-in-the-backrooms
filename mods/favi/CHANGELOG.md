# What's new in Favi

## 1.6.0
- No more size limits in Forge Files. Paste an Idea Forge file of any length (a million characters tested), write fields as long as you like, and let rounds add as many new categories as they need.
- Create from game and the Mod Market now read your whole file and plan, not just the start.

## 1.5.0
- New in Forge Files: a "Create from game" button. Favi reads your game plan so far (your Codex pages and design notes) and what's already built, writes it all into a new file, then asks you what's next.
- When the file is ready, Finalize → Build it with Savi hands it straight to Savi to build.

## 1.3.8
- Mod Market refreshed: Favi now knows all 192 mods on the market today, including 22 new ones (like story, spawn-vr, wisp-swarm, xyz-gizmo and portal-routing).
- Every mod shows its latest version, install and like counts, price, and full description.
- Mods that left the market are gone from her list, so she won't suggest something you can't install.

## 1.3.7
- Removed: Favi's tab inside the Codex window. Favi no longer touches Codex's own code. "/favi codex" still runs /codex.
- If a Favi page ever breaks, her tab now says what went wrong instead of going blank.

## 1.3.6
- Fixed: in some worlds, tapping a page in Favi's tab snapped you back to her main menu. Pages now stay open, and your taps reach her in every world.

## 1.3.5
- New: put "/autoremind" in a prompt and Favi keeps reminding Savi to finish that task within 30 seconds if she stops early. Type "/autoremind stop" to end it. Favi's reminders don't use fuel; Savi's work still does.
- New: "/autoremind crew" hands the task to Savi's crew, so the work keeps going when you close the app. A crew uses fuel.
- New: type "/favi optimize" and Favi makes your game and your mods faster and lighter, without changing how anything looks or plays.
- Add a word to aim it: "/favi loadFaster" (opens quicker), "/favi smoother" (less lag), "/favi phone" (better on phones), "/favi size" (cleans out unused stuff), "/favi mods" or "/favi game" (just one side). She tells you what was slow and how much faster it got.
- Stack checkup words in one go: "/favi loadFaster phone mods" does loading and phone checkups on your mods.
- New: type just "/favi" to see a guide to every command and checkup word.

## 1.3.4
- Fixed: Playtest Eyes getting stuck on "wrapping up" when you tapped done.
- Cancel now always stops a watch right away.

## 1.3.3
- Fixed: tapping "done, read it now" sometimes did nothing.
- Added a cancel button while she's wrapping up.
- She now shows "opening her eyes" until she's really watching.

## 1.3.2
- Fixed: the clear chat button didn't work on phones. Now you tap clear, then tap again to confirm, and the chat empties instantly.
- Fixed: deleting a Forge file had the same problem. It works the same way now.

## 1.3.1
- Fixed: Favi not answering your messages in some games.
- Fixed: Playtest Eyes not recording your play. She now shows how many seconds she's watched.
- Your messages appear in the chat the moment you send them.

## 1.3.0
- Playtest Eyes: Favi watches you play for a minute, then tells you where you got stuck, bored or lost, and how to fix it.
- Plan vs game: see how much of your Forge file is actually built.
- Daily spark: a small new idea for your game every day. Keep it or toss it.
- Moodboard: your chosen art style's pictures sit at the top of the chat.

## 1.2.0
- Favi's home screen shows her painted cover.

## 1.1.0
- Favi works in any game as soon as you install her. No setup.
- Every reply comes with three quick answers you can tap.
- Clear empties the whole chat (or type /clear).
- Tapping twice no longer sends your message twice.
- Darker, easier-to-read panel that sits high on the screen so you can use it while Savi works.

## 1.0.0
- First version: chat with Favi (she can see your game), Forge Files, reference pictures for /style, a Mod Market sorted for your game, and the /mods, /favi and /uninstall commands.
