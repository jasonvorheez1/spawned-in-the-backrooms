# Aqua

## Who
One of the 7 Colored Flowers from Asgore and Toriel's wedding bouquet, brought to life by the Flower Kingdom dark world. JP name ターコイズ (Turquoise). Her trait is patience, which she has none of. Recurring miniboss, almost always with Seth, who named her and tries to keep her in check.
Playful, childish, naive and very violent. She can't tell fighting from friendship, gets bored of doing the same thing twice, and doesn't understand that people can die. Echoes Jevil (chaos, laughter) and mirrors Kris (skin tone, dark bangs over red eyes, a knife, blue).
> "I was hoping being human was about playing since I always wanted to play a game."
Her talking-face icon is a cyan knife.

## Look sheet
- **silhouette**: a wide bell dress, a big black hair mass whose sides curl up and out like bat wings or devil horns, and a red heart bow on top. She reads at 20 m from those three shapes.
- **proportions**: chibi, about 2.5 heads tall. Second shortest flower (only Orange is shorter). Head nearly as wide as the dress hem.
- **palette** (sampled from the overworld sprite, `-unique-colors`):
  - `#55FFFF` cyan: skin, dress, sleeves
  - `#186062` dark teal: hair edge highlights, dress shading
  - `#ED1C24` red: heart bow, neck band, shoes
  - `#000000` black: hair, outlines, smile
  - `#FFFFFF` white: the eye(s) when revealed
- **signature details**: bangs hide her eyes completely. She shows one big eye, or three in a row, with red irises. Red band around her neck. Red shoes. Long flared sleeves. The red ribbons are homemade from Green's handkerchief and red food colouring, because one colour is boring.
- **variants**: dress soaked red (twirl gif), green ribbon in Castle West, wide grin showing one tooth (smiling at Asgore).

## Motion & moves
- **idle / soaked twirl gif** (7 frames): she spins in place with the dress flaring wide, one arm flung out and then the other, hair swinging. She never stands still.
- **Knife fan** (215-frame gif): four knives thrown across the board, then they boomerang back at the SOUL, about 7 or 8 times, with grey petals falling behind.
- **Knife chain**: 16 knives, 8 from the top and 8 from the bottom, close on the SOUL in a curved wave that looks like a closing jaw, about 6 times, while she sways back and forth.
- **Knife circle**: rings of knives appear at random spots and burst outward, 11 or 12 rings per attack.
- **Omega knife**: grids of big knives spin and drift toward the SOUL for 16 seconds, then slow down.
- **Duck**: a slow duck crosses the board and falls over. If it would kill you, it grows a muscular arm and crushes the SOUL to *Thrash Machine*.
- **Everything**: fan, chain and circle at once, only if you were never hit in the first fight.
- Damage 62 base, softened after the 7th death (−5 each, down to 42).
- ACTs: she copies Kris (a Check back, a spin, a dance).

## Video summaries
- **"Aqua Boss Fight - Deltarune Chapter 5"** (youtube 84QWo1-NwQ4, 6:05, by FazBusiness). *Read from 16 storyboard frames across the whole video; YouTube gave no transcript.* 0:25–1:35: in the Garden under the rose arch, Aqua stands centre-top facing Kris, Susie and Ralsei. Ralsei opens ("...hello there"), Susie hesitates ("...hey... uh..."), and Aqua says "being human is about..." playing. Ralsei offers "How about..." a game. 2:00: the battle starts ("A mysterious festival begins"). Kris ACTs a dance and "the enemy danced, too!": she mirrors Kris. 3:15–3:40: her knife-fan and knife-chain patterns, with Kris's HP dropping to about 135 of 240. 4:05: she shouts "My way!" and the fight runs to about 77% TP. 4:50–5:40: back in the Garden she spares them: "Come to think of it..." and "I know! Behind me is a really special feather!" Susie: "Duh. You can trust us." It ends on the cyan Deltarune end card. **Lesson for the build:** she's won by playing along, not by beating her. She copies your moves, her knives come in readable patterns, and the "special feather" is her reward hook.
- **"Deltarune Chapter 5 - Full Aqua Boss Fight"** (youtube DtGCs9FM48Y). *From frames only.* It opens with the same Garden encounter, but Kris is flanked by two yellow figures (Yellow's trial-era cameo, or a later re-encounter). It moves to the battle (Kris vs Aqua, knives closing) and back to the Garden after the win. **Lesson:** the Garden arch with the knife fan above it is her stage, her signature backdrop.

## For the build
- **model**: a scripted figure about 1.25 m tall. Bell dress, horn-curl hair, heart bow, red collar and shoes, cyan knife in hand. Refs: the overworld sheet plus the Garden screenshot.
- **must read**: (1) the horn-curl hair silhouette, (2) the cyan + red + black palette, (3) the knife.
- **animation**: a restless sway now. Later, the twirl from the soaked gif.
- **if it becomes a fight**: knives in fans, chains (closing jaw) and rings. Her rose-arch garden with a knife fan overhead is the stage.
- **simplified on purpose**: no pixel outlines on the 3D model, and the eyes are hidden by default (params.eyes 0 / 1 / 3).
