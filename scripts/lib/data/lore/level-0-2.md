# Level 0.2 "Remodeled Mess" (Wikidot, survival difficulty 3)
Shelly handed this page on Nov 12 (#242xxx) and asked for the level plus a quest with a physics puzzle.

- A sub-section between Level 0 and Level 1, made by Backrooms Remodeling Co. trying to "remodel" Level 0.
- Untouched, it looks fixed: dry red carpet, repainted white walls, working Wi-Fi and outlets, the odd bathroom.
- Once a wanderer enters it starts to fall apart: ceiling tiles drop, walls topple, the floor caves in and shows an
  unfinished house underneath. Debris and the dust after are the danger.
- The chain reaction stops when it reaches the door back to Level 0. Re-entered, the level is whole again.
- Entry: in Level 0, where the buzz quiets, the carpet dries and rooms look under construction, a door may appear.
- Exit: find a door, it leads back to Level 0.
- No bases, outposts or communities.
- Recovered documents (clipboard): 1) "Backrooms Remodelling Company. True order comes through furniture."
  2) Major Took's job notes to Task Force 102-CX (Target Level 0, Sector A880-1, Class-A Remodeling Job): do good this
  time or Cap'n gets punished. 3) A cheerful, rambling letter to Major Took from a crew member (Penelope's voice),
  "everything is a-ok", asking for more chances; the rest is torn off.
