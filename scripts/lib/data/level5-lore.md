# Level 5 · "Terror Hotel" (Wikidot, as Shelly handed it, condensed for the builders)
Survival difficulty: Class 2-3 (unsafe, mostly secure; entities rare in the halls, common in the Boiler Room).
A grand 1920s-30s hotel with no outside: art-deco and Victorian rooms, damask wallpaper, brass and mahogany, chandeliers,
an endless sense of being watched. The air smells of old perfume and cigars. Time feels wrong: clocks disagree.
Wanderers report growing paranoia and dread the longer they stay (sanity drain), worst in the Boiler Room.

## Areas
- The Main Hall: the lobby (front desk, bell, key cubbies, chesterfields, potted palms, two crystal chandeliers), a long
  gallery and the guest wings. Room doors carry gold numbers that follow no order; most rooms are ordinary, made beds,
  luggage, a radio; some hold a single odd thing. The halls are mostly safe.
- The Beverly Room: the Eternal Ballroom. A huge ballroom where a party seems to have stopped a moment ago: set tables,
  half-full champagne coupes, a cake, a bandstand with instruments and nobody playing them. Music drifts from nowhere
  (here: radios playing a violin polonaise). Wanderers hear laughter and glasses clinking from empty corners.
  Samantha holds court here.
- The Boiler Room: deep, loud, unbearably hot. Rows of boilers, furnaces, pipes, valves, gauges, catwalks. Steam bursts.
  Many entities (Death Rats, Hounds, Humanoids). Its far doors lead to Level 6 (unbearable heat behind) and other levels.

## Ways in / out (this game)
In: the guest lift from Level 3 (lobby west wall), the old stairway from Level 4 (north guest row).
Out (sealed in this build, they say why): Level 9 (dark wooden door, lobby), Level 98 (warm metal door, lobby),
Level 63 (birdsong behind it, Beverly Room), Level 84 (barred gate, Beverly Room), Level 6 (burning handle, Boiler Room),
Level 78 (airlock, Boiler Room). Elevators: floors 1-12 are this same hotel (another bank of lifts); 13 and up don't come back.

## Factions (this game's cast; Wikidot groups adapted to an MMO)
- "Housekeeping": an M.E.G. outpost in the old laundry south of the ballroom. Agents in pressed grey; they trade, heal,
  guide the way into the Boiler Room, post jobs (clear the rats, bleed the valves). Tired, professional, gallows humour.
- Homely Hotel: a group who decided to run the hotel as if it still had guests: a concierge at the front desk, a cook in
  the dining room, a bellhop, a maid. Uncanny-polite hospitality; they rent you a room (a key) for almond water; inside,
  you can rest (sanity back) and stash things. Never, ever rude to a guest. They don't like the Originals.
- The Originals: old-timers in the salon off the ballroom who swear they were here "before the hotel went wrong": formal,
  dressed for 1931, eccentric, card games and gossip, they trade odd heirlooms and lore, and they keep Samantha fed.

## Samantha (entity)
A large, sleek cat, black with gold eyes, intelligent and psychic. She speaks (in your head, quiet, amused).
She asks for meat. Fed, she gives a reading: a prediction or piece of advice about you and the levels; the better the meat,
the truer and more useful the reading. Cheap meat gets a vague or teasing answer. Provoked (struck, robbed, or
unfed after promising meat), she turns violent: very fast, very dangerous, then calm again once you leave or feed her.

## Entities
- Death Rats: rat-sized but vicious, in packs, eyes that glow; they swarm, bite fast for small damage, flee light/fire.
  They nest in the Boiler Room and come up through vents. Killing them gives rat meat (poor meat for Samantha).
- Hounds: as on other levels (crawl on all fours, hunt by sound), rarer here, mostly the Boiler Room.
- Humanoids: tall faceless figures in old hotel uniforms ("The Staff" to wanderers): they stand still in corridors
  facing walls, follow when you turn your back, strike hard when you're alone. Slow; you can outpace them.
