# Backrooms MMORPG (working title)

A persistent, shared Level 0. Players scavenge, fight and level up in an endless yellow maze, and push deeper in groups.
The tone is dread first: you start weak and the place is bigger than you.

## Decisions
- Camera: first person by default, with a toggle to third person (key V). Third person must not clip through walls or ceiling.
- Combat: survival combat. Players start bare-handed (weak punches) and need a weapon fast.
- Death: players keep their items. Not a hardcore game. Death costs time (respawn in the hub), not progress.
- Guns: rare loot first; crafting and player/NPC trading come later.
- Progress: saved per player (player.state), not shared across the world.
- First-push scope: not decided yet (default: Phase 1 first).

- 2026-11-07: Smilers follow the wiki: a lit flashlight draws them from ~40 m (11 m dark); eye contact slows them to a creep. Shelly said yes.
- 2026-11-07: Level 0 wiki additions (Shelly: "i want all of it"): stairs to nowhere, walls that shift behind you (arch rooms never), deep wet carpet slows you in arch rooms, arch ledges restore stamina/sanity, hum spikes drain sanity while standing still, whispers/scratching behind walls, dark figures down long halls.

## Power curve
| Stage | Player | Weapons and tools | Threat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | Weak, slow stamina, fists only | Found melee: table leg, pipe scrap, crowbar, bat. Primitive tools: flashlight, rope, lighter | Smilers one at a time; sanity is the main enemy |
| Mid | Levels 10-25, better stats | Pipe weapons, hatchet, pistol, shotgun; ammo is scarce | Packs, new enemy types, dark and red zones |
| Late | Built like a soldier: armor, rifles, high hp | Rifle, body armor, night vision, explosives | Deep levels, bosses, events with many enemies |

Weapons come from loot and crafting, not only from level. Level gates the stats and what you can equip, so a new player can't just be handed a gun.

## Phases (each playable alone)
1. Core loop: stats, HUD, fists, sprint, almond water, smilers, sanity by zone.
2. RPG: XP and levels, loot, an inventory, weapon tiers, death rules.
3. MMO: shared hub, chat, leaderboard, co-op (revive, sanity drains slower near others), enemy count scales with players.
4. Depth: Level 1+, exit doors, hub quests, rare events.

## Direction: 7 Days to Die / voxel-style play (user request)
Take the mechanics, not the assets: a block world you can dig and build in, scavenging and crafting, a base to defend, and a day/night threat clock with waves of enemies.
- Voxel building and digging (engine has voxel terrain: see the voxel-terrain skill). Open question: the whole world voxel, or Level 0 stays a maze and a new voxel level is added.
- Crafting from scavenged parts (ties into weapons: rare guns, crafted pipe weapons).
- Base defense: placeable barricades and traps, with periodic smiler hordes that test the base.
- Day/night or "the lights fail" timer that raises the threat.

- 2026-11-07 weapon system overhaul (Shelly: make stats matter, real ballistics, new real-world weapons in split loot pools, correct two-handed holds). **Ballistics** (`scripts/lib/ballistics.js`): a gun no longer picks targets in a cone; every shot is real bullets (one per pellet). Each leaves the eye along the look plus a random angle (`acc [hip, ads]` degrees in weapons.yml, wider when moving, jumping or worn, narrower crouched or aimed, opened by a held trigger), flies in 6 m legs with gravity and drag (`mv` muzzle velocity m/s, `drag` per metre, `zero` the sight-in range: the real numbers for each cartridge, so a .38 drops about a metre at 100 m and a rifle round barely drops), and stops at the first body or wall it meets. Damage falls with the speed a bullet has lost and past the weapon's range; a hit above the shoulder is a HEADSHOT (x2); corpses and floor items are no cover. A wall takes a bullet hole (a dark disc that stays 40 s) and a spark and dust puff; every bullet draws a tracer streak. **Stats matter** (`handling` in `scripts/lib/stats.js`): weapons.yml `req: { h: Hardiness, guns | melee: skill }` is what a weapon asks of you. Short of it, a gun spreads wider (+17% per Hardiness point, +1.2% per skill point), kicks harder, wanders off the aim, sags in the first-person hold and comes up to the eye slower, and does a little less damage; a melee weapon costs more stamina, swings slower and hits softer. Stronger than it asks steadies it a little. Guns skill always tightens the spread (x1.2 at 0, x0.8 at 100). The K sheet shows what the equipped weapon needs and what you are short; inventory lines show REQ. **New weapons** (all real-world): golf club, tire iron, pickaxe, side-handle baton (PR-24), meat cleaver, .38 revolver, coach gun (double-barrel 12 ga), lever-action rifle (.30-30), bolt-action hunting rifle (.308 with scope), M.E.G. carbine (AR-15 pattern, 5.56). Rifles load one round at a time (`tube: true`). **Loot pools are split** (`pools:` in weapons.yml; `rollWeapon(ctx, pool)` and `poolOf(place)` in `scripts/lib/loot.js`): a pooled weapon is rolled only in its place; l0 lobby: golf club; l1: tire iron, lever rifle; l2: pickaxe; l3 (station, Gamma, horde, M.E.G. bodies): pickaxe, hunting rifle, carbine; l4 offices: baton, revolver; l5 hotel: cleaver, revolver, coach gun. Marlo in Gamma sells the revolver and the carbine. Kills drop from the pool of the place they die in. **Two-handed holds**: weapons.yml `two: [up, forward, radius]` puts the off hand (a second arm, `scripts/viewmodel-geo.js` support hand) on the fore-end of a long gun or up the haft of a sledgehammer, axe, pickaxe, bat or golf club, and brings the weapon toward the centre of the screen. Not yet seen drawn in a live camera: the new weapon models, the support hand, tracers and bullet holes were only checked as loading without fault, and the shot path was checked by calling the ballistics directly (hits, headshots, drop), not by pressing the trigger.
